worker oom, june

Workers kept dying at the end of June because the month-end batch pulled the whole month of orders into memory. Confirmations were about forty minutes late and support got around twenty tickets. Jonas found the batch flag after an hour.

1. kubectl -n shop top pod -l app=orders-worker and look for pods near 512Mi
2. kubectl -n shop get pods -l app=orders-worker and check the restart count
3. kubectl -n shop rollout restart deploy/orders-worker
4. if it keeps happening, kubectl -n shop rollout undo deploy/orders-worker
