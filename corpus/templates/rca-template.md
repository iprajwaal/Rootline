## Analysis

### 5-whys analysis

1st why
- Question: Why did the issue occur?
- Answer: The direct cause of the incident.

2nd why
- Question: Why did this cause occur?
- Answer: The reason behind the direct cause.

3rd why
- Question: Why did this underlying reason occur?
- Answer: The system, process or people issue that allowed it.

4th why
- Question: Why was this issue not prevented?
- Answer: Gaps in monitoring, automation, testing or other safeguards.

5th why
- Question: Why does this gap exist?
- Answer: The root cause, such as a process flaw, missing training or missing tooling.
