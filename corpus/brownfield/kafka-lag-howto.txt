fix consumer lag on the orders queue by scaling consumers
(copied from the old wiki, owner: orders team, last checked 2026-05-19)

Use this procedure when the OrderQueueConsumerLagHigh alert fires, or when worker logs show "consumer group orders-worker lag=... on topic orders exceeds threshold=10000".

before you start: kubectl access to the shop namespace. The orders topic has 6 partitions. More than 6 consumers adds nothing.

steps
1) Check the consumer lag.
   kafka-consumer-groups --bootstrap-server order-queue:9092 --describe --group orders-worker
   should see: Lag above 10000 on one or more partitions.
2) Check worker memory before scaling.
   kubectl -n shop top pod -l app=orders-worker
   should see: Memory under 80% of the limit and no recent restarts. If a worker is near its limit or restarting, stop here and follow the orders-worker OOM runbook.
3) Check the worker logs for slow payment calls.
   kubectl -n shop logs deploy/orders-worker --since=15m
   should see: No payment-gateway timeouts. If payment calls are slow, scaling helps little. Page the payments team on-call and continue.
4) Scale the consumers.
   kubectl -n shop scale deploy/orders-worker --replicas=4
   should see: 4 worker pods running and every partition assigned.
5) Check the lag again after five minutes.
   kafka-consumer-groups --bootstrap-server order-queue:9092 --describe --group orders-worker
   should see: Lag is going down on every partition.

to undo
- Scale back to the normal size once lag is under 1000. kubectl -n shop scale deploy/orders-worker --replicas=2
