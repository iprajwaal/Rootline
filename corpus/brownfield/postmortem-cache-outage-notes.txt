Postmortem: cache outage notes
(copied from the old wiki, owner: platform team, last checked 2026-02-03)

Use this procedure when the OrdersApiHighLatency alert fires and orders-api logs show "redis.exceptions.ConnectionError: Error 111 connecting to cache:6379. Connection refused."

before you start: kubectl access to the shop namespace.

steps
1) Confirm the cache does not answer.
   redis-cli -h cache ping
   should see: Connection refused instead of PONG.
2) Confirm orders-api is falling back to the database.
   kubectl -n shop logs deploy/orders-api --since=15m
   should see: ConnectionError lines for cache:6379.
3) Restart the cache.
   kubectl -n shop rollout restart statefulset/cache
   should see: The cache pod is running and ready.
4) Confirm the cache answers again.
   redis-cli -h cache ping
   should see: PONG.

if this does not help: page the platform on-call if the cache pod is not ready 10 minutes after step 3.
