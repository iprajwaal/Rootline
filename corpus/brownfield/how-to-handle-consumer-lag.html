How to handle consumer lag on the orders queue

Created by Luis Romero, last modified on 2025-08-19

Summary

A flash sale sent four times the normal order volume. Two workers could not keep up and the orders topic built a backlog of 52,000 messages. Orders were taken but confirmed up to 25 minutes late.

What happened

During a flash sale the orders topic received about 1,200 messages per second against a normal 300. Two orders-worker replicas processed about 700 per second, so consumer lag grew to 52,000 and order confirmations were delayed.

Why it happened

  • Worker capacity was fixed at normal traffic levels and the sale was not planned for.
  • The lag procedure in use restarted workers, which added no capacity.

Follow-ups

  • Replace SOP-QUEUE-001 with a procedure that scales consumers. (Luis Romero)
  • Add sales events to the capacity calendar. (Marketing team)
  • Autoscale workers on consumer lag. (Orders team)
