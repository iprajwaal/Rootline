Slow checkout while the cache was down

Created by Omar Haddad, last modified on 2025-10-24

Summary

The cache pod was evicted during a node drain and did not come back on its own. orders-api fell back to the database for every read, and checkout p95 latency rose to 2.8 seconds for 22 minutes.

What happened

Checkout p95 latency rose from 250 ms to 2.8 s. orders-api logged "redis.exceptions.ConnectionError: Error 111 connecting to cache:6379. Connection refused." The cache pod was stuck in Pending after a node drain.

Why it happened

  • The cache ran as a single pod with no PodDisruptionBudget, so a planned drain took it down.
  • Its volume was bound to one node, so the pod could not move to another node.

Follow-ups

  • Add a PodDisruptionBudget for the cache. (Platform team)
  • Alert when redis-cli ping fails for 2 minutes. (SRE team)
  • Write SOP-CACHE-001. (Omar Haddad)
