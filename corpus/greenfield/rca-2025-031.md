## Problem statement

Checkout p95 latency rose from 250 ms to 2.8 s. orders-api logged "redis.exceptions.ConnectionError: Error 111 connecting to cache:6379. Connection refused." The cache pod was stuck in Pending after a node drain.

## Impact

### Description of impact

External customers in the EU region saw slow checkout. No requests failed.

### Key metrics affected

22 minutes of slow checkout. Conversion dropped about 4% during the window. No SLA breach.

### Customer feedback

No tickets. Night-time traffic was low.

## Immediate resolution

Restarted the cache statefulset after the drained node came back.

## Analysis

### 5-whys analysis

1st why

- Question: Why was checkout slow?
- Answer: orders-api could not reach the cache and read everything from orders-db.

2nd why

- Question: Why could it not reach the cache?
- Answer: The only cache pod was evicted during a node drain.

3rd why

- Question: Why did the drain evict the only pod?
- Answer: The cache statefulset had no PodDisruptionBudget.

4th why

- Question: Why did the pod not start again on another node?
- Answer: Its volume was bound to the drained node.

5th why

- Question: Why was this not found earlier?
- Answer: There was no alert on the cache itself, and drains did not check for single replica services.

### Fishbone analysis

#### Environment

- Single cache replica with a node-bound volume.

#### Process

- Node drains run without a check for single replica services.

#### Data

- No alert on cache availability, only on orders-api latency.

## Root causes

Based on the analysis above, the root causes of the incident are:

1. The cache ran as a single pod with no PodDisruptionBudget, so a planned drain took it down.
2. Its volume was bound to one node, so the pod could not move to another node.
